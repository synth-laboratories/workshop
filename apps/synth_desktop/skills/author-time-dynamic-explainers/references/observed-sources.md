Cross-example observations:

- The object being taught is the graphic vocabulary.
- Most frames have one dominant transformation and very little chrome.
- Complexity accumulates through time, repetition, splitting, or scale.
- Color is semantic and sparse; outlines remain crisp.
- Motion preserves spatial identity when continuity teaches, and cuts to a new
  scene when explanatory scale changes.
