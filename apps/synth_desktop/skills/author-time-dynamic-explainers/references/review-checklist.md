# Review checklist

Inspect the actual wide and compact PNGs. JSON validity is not visual approval.

## Teaching

- Can a viewer state the mechanism and its defining transformation without the
  transcript?
- Does every beat add one necessary fact?
- Is the state before and after each transformation clear?
- Is the final/poster frame meaningful on its own?

## Composition

- Is there one dominant focal relationship per beat?
- Are there at most two simultaneous independent motions?
- Are labels adjacent to their objects and connectors unambiguous?
- Is negative space intentional and balanced?
- Are repeated objects aligned and evenly spaced?
- Do the largest sections correspond to real systems or responsibilities?
- Does the dominant mechanism receive the most visual space?
- Are full populations and changing subsets separately legible?

## Information flow

- Can the viewer identify the object moving across every important boundary?
- Do queue, worker, record, and trace sections contain actual objects rather
  than explanatory labels alone?
- Are cross-section paths short, orthogonal, and free of records, labels,
  charts, and unrelated boundaries?
- Does returned evidence become durable state before a later decision reads it?
- Does missing evidence remain visibly missing rather than zero or complete?

## Typography

- Is all essential text at least 12 screen pixels in both captures?
- Are labels concise, horizontal, and free of clipping or collision?
- Can the current beat caption be read before the scene advances?

## Motion

- Does motion show data, control, scale, or state changing?
- Do object identities and causal paths remain trackable?
- Does each transition settle before the next begins?
- Does reduced motion retain the lesson?
- Were setup, in-flight work, decision/update, and settled-result captures all
  inspected rather than approving only the poster?

## Restraint

- Remove any container, legend, step number, glow, or label that does not teach.
- Reject a completed flowchart that only changes opacity.
- Reject a compact capture that succeeds only by shrinking the wide scene.
