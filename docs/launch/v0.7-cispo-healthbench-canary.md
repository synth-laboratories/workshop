## CISPO ruling

This run **does not validate hosted CISPO**. It used Tinker's native group-relative `importance_sampling` RLVR objective, while the hosted catalog requires implementation identity `cispo.slime.v1`. The trained student also failed the teacher-quality regression gate. Neither the observed RLVR uplift nor the checkpoint may change `openai/gpt-oss-20b` CISPO from `not_validated` to `available` or `preview`.

The next valid CISPO canary must provide the registered implementation identity, an immutable training-state checkpoint in addition to a sampler checkpoint, and a frozen heldout result that clears every configured safety gate.

The provider proof was not interrupted deliberately: it needed a terminal heldout result. Cancellation and recovery are covered separately by the hosted client contract suite (`tests/test_hosted_training.py`, 14 passed on 2026-08-19), including a resumed training attempt with a fresh tunnel lease and backend-authoritative checkpoint reference.
